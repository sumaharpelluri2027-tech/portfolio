About

An engineer who learned to sell.

I trained as a mechanical engineer at VIT, where I designed the hybrid braking system for our BAJA SAE car. We finished eighth in India. That project taught me to think in systems: every part has inputs, failure modes and a person who depends on it.

At Myntra and Wipro I learned that data only matters when it changes a decision. At Rabbit Invest, as a founding member, I learned the harder part: building the operations function, writing the SOPs, automating the repetitive work and being accountable for the numbers in front of the founders.

Now, at Masters’ Union, I build things on the side because that’s how I learn. BoardIQ, Decco Store, the Wing’s website and a voice agent named Priya all started as questions I wanted to answer with something real. I’m looking for a Data/BI, Founder’s Office or Product role where I can do the same at scale.

How I work
01

Frame the decision

Start with what must change, not which tool looks interesting.

02

Trace the mess

Map inputs, owners, handoffs and delays. The opportunities hide between teams.

03

Make data earn trust

Validate sources and show a confidence level before any intelligence.

04

Design the loop

Observe, decide, act, record, and know when to ask a human.

05

Ship, measure, tighten

Release the smallest complete loop and turn each exception into a better system.
